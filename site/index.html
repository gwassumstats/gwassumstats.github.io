What is new in the files?

  1. An added dataset has its GWAS Catalog accession (GCST…) as ID, and New 2026 as release.
  2. It has an h² file and, if signed, an rg file: the same without strand-ambiguous SNPs. M counts the SNPs of the h² file.
  3. N is the sample size of the study; the N of a SNP in the file can differ.
  4. 26 datasets are unsigned, 3 of them added in 2026: they have h² but no rg.

How were h² and rg updated?

  1. h² of the added datasets was estimated as in 2024. GCST90503316 uses a corrected estimate (h² 0.0945, SE 0.009).
  2. rg of the pairs of 2024 is unchanged. Every other pair of signed datasets of the same ancestry was estimated in the same way. No pair has one dataset of each ancestry.

Which pairs have no rg?

LDSC gave no estimate for 821 of the 134,940 EUR pairs and 55 of the 1,540 EAS pairs; the site shows them as not estimated. A few datasets account for most: GCST90705031 lacks an estimate with 518 of the 519 other EUR datasets, and GCST90320065 with 55 of the 55 other EAS datasets.

How were the nonredundant panels chosen?

  1. The dataset with the most pairs without an estimate is removed, again and again, until every pair left has one: 22 EUR datasets and 1 EAS dataset go, 498 and 55 stay.
  2. Of those, the datasets with h² z > 6 are taken from the highest z down. One enters the panel if its squared rg with every dataset already in it is at most 0.1.

The EUR panel keeps 66 of its 74 datasets of 2024, and 16 of its datasets were added in 2026. The EAS panel keeps 11 of 12, with 6 added in 2026. Step 1 is new: the rule of 2024 dropped any dataset with a pair without an estimate, which now is every dataset.

What is not in it?

GWAS whose sumstats are outside the GWAS Catalog, and papers with more than 100 accessions, are not added yet.

About GWAS Sumstats

A catalog of the Price lab's collection of GWAS summary statistics: 602 datasets in the format of LDSC, with their heritability (h²) and their genetic correlations (rg). The files are on the O2 cluster; lab members log in to see where. The code is in the public repository.

What is in a file?

One row per SNP, tab-separated, in the .sumstats format of LDSC. Only HapMap3 SNPs.

ColumnContent

A1, A2The two alleles; the sign of Z is for A1

In the 26 unsigned datasets, Z has no direction; 3 of them have no A1 and A2.

Which file do I use for h² and for rg?

  1. Datasets of 2024 and before: one file, for both.
  2. Datasets added in 2026: the h² file for h², the rg file for rg. The rg file is the h² file without strand-ambiguous SNPs (A/T and C/G).
  3. Unsigned datasets: h² only.

A dataset's page lists its files after login.

How were h² and rg estimated?

  1. h²: stratified LD score regression (S-LDSC) with the baseline-LD model v2.2, on the observed scale. Its z is h² over its standard error. The intercept is that of the regression.
  2. rg: cross-trait LD score regression, for every pair of signed datasets of the same ancestry.
  3. Both use LD scores of 1000 Genomes Phase 3 for the dataset's ancestry, EUR or EAS.

What is the nonredundant panel?

85 EUR and 17 EAS datasets with h² z > 6 and a squared rg of at most 0.1 between any two: traits that are close to independent. The release notes say how they were chosen.

How do I cite it?

Cite the paper of each dataset you use: its reference. For h² and rg, cite LDSC (Bulik-Sullivan et al. 2015), cross-trait LDSC (Bulik-Sullivan et al. 2015), S-LDSC (Finucane et al. 2015) and the baseline-LD model (Gazal et al. 2017).
